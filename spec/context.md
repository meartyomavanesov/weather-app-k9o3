The weather app is a mobile application designed to provide users with accurate and timely weather information, catering to outdoor enthusiasts, travelers, and anyone needing to plan their day based on weather conditions. Users can access real-time updates, view hourly and daily forecasts, and receive alerts for severe weather events. The app allows customization by saving favorite locations, exploring interactive radar maps, and accessing detailed data on temperature, humidity, wind speed, and precipitation. By delivering reliable weather data, the app empowers users to make informed decisions about their daily activities, enhancing safety and convenience.

- Real-time weather updates
- Hourly and daily forecasts
- Severe weather alerts
- Customizable favorite locations
- Interactive radar maps
- Detailed information on temperature, humidity, and wind speed
- User-friendly interface for easy navigation
- Offline access to saved weather data
